# Team roles

Northstar Desk supports four roles: Owner, Editor, Commenter, and Viewer.

## What each role may do

An Owner may change billing and may delete the workspace. An Editor may create and
change documents but may not change billing. A Commenter may add comments but may not
change a document. A Viewer may read documents only.

## Constraints

Every workspace must have at least one Owner. The last Owner of a workspace cannot be
removed or downgraded. Only an Owner may invite a new member.
