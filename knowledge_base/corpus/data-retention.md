# Data retention

A deleted Northstar Desk project remains recoverable for 30 days. After 30 days the
project and its documents are removed permanently and cannot be restored.

## Exports

An export of a workspace contains every document that was current at the time of the
export. Exports are retained for 7 days and are then deleted automatically.

## Audit records

Audit records are retained for 365 days. An audit record must never be deleted before
its retention period ends, even when the project it belongs to was removed.
